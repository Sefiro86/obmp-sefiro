animations on your character

    drag: orbit and raise
    wheel: zoom

    Controls

    Mouse and keyboard. Letters only type into the search box while it is active (click it or press Ctrl+F; Esc leaves it). ? or Esc closes this page.

    Choose

    ←→
    tab (or click the tab)
    ↑↓
    animation (Page Up and Page Down to jump); double-click to play it
    Ctrl+F
    search by name
    *
    add to or remove from favorites

    Play

    Enter
    play the selected animation
    Space
    pause and resume
    +−
    speed, from 0.1× to 2×
    F1
    loop
    F2
    stop
    F7 ⌂
    go back to where you were when you opened the menu (after animations that move you)
    M
    root motion on or off: the animation moves your character, or it stays in place
    F8
    tour: play every animation from the selection down
    Esc
    close the menu

    Study

    ,.
    one frame back or forward
    timeline
    click: jump to that point
    []
    mark the start and the end of the section to repeat
    \
    clear the section

    Camera

    AD
    orbit (hold)
    WS
    raise and lower
    QE
    zoom in and out
    1…6
    framing: three-quarter, front, profile, back, close-up, feet
    R
    starting framing, on the spot where you are now
    T
    slow continuous turntable
    pad
    drag to orbit and raise, wheel to zoom

    Animations play on your own character, so switch to third person. The other players do not see them yet. When you close the menu (Esc or Alt+Y) the animation stops and you can move normally again.
